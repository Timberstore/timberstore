## Fixes

- **Desktop contacts:** Apollo span padding and our anchor padding added up instead of forming a real icon/text gap. Native phone/envelope pseudo glyphs now participate in the same inline-flex group as the native link, with an actual **8 px gap**, centered vertically. Both padding sources and the native pseudo transform are neutralized in the existing checkout component. Contact-list column gap is 18 px, socials retain their own styling and sit at the end of the available list space. Outer desktop surface remains 920×52 at tested widths ≥1280 px; heading, hours, destinations, icon fonts/colors and social links are retained. This is scoped to checkout; the general website service bar is untouched.
- **Mobile contacts:** two equal columns at all tested phone widths; centered native glyph above each contact, 6 px vertical gap and a central divider. At 320 px local 11 px contact text fits; otherwise 12 px. Existing stretched native links cover the glyph and text; no new click handler or copied contacts. Reset the remaining native translateY(-50%) on the now-static glyph, which otherwise lifted it by 10 px. Heading and dot+hours remain centered, mobile socials hidden.
- **Green and white text:** main positive token remains `#439d29`. Active-step counters now use white **700 / 20 px** numerals consistently, including hover/focus. The common contrast text variant remains `#31771c`; it is used for small stock/free/shipping-price text. The new CTA surface token is `#3a841f`, aliasing through `--ts-color-checkout-cta`; the slightly deeper, aligned shade supports ordinary small white text as well as the large label (**4.67:1**). All of the final CTA now uses that uniform surface. Removed the suffix background/padding/radius at its original rule, leaving a transparent suffix with white text and a small normal line gap. No overlay/pseudo patch was added. Main-green white counters meet the large-text threshold at 3.45:1. Disabled opacity/native semantics remain; focus has a distinct outline. Hover retains sufficient contrast with the existing brightness effect.
- **Personal data:** retained the measured symmetric heading/login alignment and restored white login text in normal/hover/focus/active states. Original orange background `#d4884a` and native login URL/form logic remain. White-on-original-orange is the requested appearance; this is not a claim of AA contrast for that orange login button.
- **Step-3 recap cards:** step 2 places `.recapitulation-single` under `#shipping-billing-summary`; step 3 server-renders them inside another `.cart-items` and leaves that ID empty. The old ID-dependent selector skipped those rows. The central selector now identifies both verified native structures through their recap method data attributes; the existing idempotent renderer applies the same two shared classes/labels. Native direct labels are hidden when the shared label is present, avoiding duplicated captions. Both steps use the same orange-accent shells, wrapping/grid and green shipping/free-payment prices. No amount/format, product, form or ordering logic is rewritten.
- **Responsive transitions:** native all-property transitions interpolated sidebar widths and CTA widths when moving between desktop and mobile, briefly squeezing the legal line. Columns now change geometry directly; CTA transition is limited to its hover filter. Static summary behavior and bounded 55vh desktop product-list scroll remain, with no new timer, observer or event handler.
